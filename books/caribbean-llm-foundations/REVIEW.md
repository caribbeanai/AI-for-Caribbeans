## Applied review procedures

The [BootLoops verification toolkit](https://github.com/BootLoops-ai/bootloops/tree/95dfb7251f00d05a1c5a5184bde1a2ce5ebad270) and [protocols](https://github.com/BootLoops-ai/skills/tree/ca892277dcf0468d995f0036f3bd6d753a8afe7d) informed independent recomputation, mutation controls, the reference audit, prose checks and a fresh-context simulated referee. The ordinary calculations in this book use Python's exact rational and decimal arithmetic. Review led to corrections before the files were rebuilt. The simulated review does not represent external peer review or consultation with every language community.

The search-only labs use dated, fictional records. No model was trained or benchmarked for this edition. The public statistics examples reproduce a small set of displayed values. Source definitions and coverage control their interpretation.

## Revised first edition (1.1)

The revision gives the reader a role and a choice at the start of each chapter. The final decision check returns to that choice. The first story gives the reader authority to set priorities, ask for more foot traffic counts and sign a lease. Later chapters separate what the reader can approve from what a dispatcher, water team or record owner controls.

Two independent simulated first reads reviewed the English and Spanish stories for a 13-year-old beginner. They checked whether a reader could name their job, identify what they could decide, follow the example and see when to ask for help. The revision defines new terms in context, works through the ordinary arithmetic first and marks deeper notation with *More maths* or *Más matemáticas*. The reading check used simulated readers. The local language samples are classroom examples, with their meanings and source notes nearby.

The final text passed all 183 arithmetic and data comparisons. The nine ferry outcomes, five negative controls and 53 place cases also passed. The verification now checks the dispatch issuer and both departure times shown in the revised lookup, the explicit capstone query times and the full arithmetic for the reply-time example.

Both final PDFs were rebuilt with XeLaTeX and rendered for page-by-page review. The English edition has 49 pages and the Spanish edition has 50. Both have 60 bookmarks and a two-page answer key. The final checks found no unresolved references, missing glyphs, text outside the trim margin or broken internal destinations.
