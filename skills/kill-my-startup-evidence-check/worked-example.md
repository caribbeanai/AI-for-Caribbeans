# Worked synthetic example

All people, figures and events below are invented teaching inputs. This is an authored expected response, not a live model trial.

Synthetic case: A founder proposes a guest-message service for small hotels. One service unit is one guest request answered and approved by a human reviewer. The proposed price is US$20 per completed unit. Variable costs are US$3 model calls, US$1 retry allowance, US$1 storage/monitoring and US$3 human review: total US$8. Contribution is US$12, or 60% of revenue, before acquisition, fixed costs and taxes. No paying customers have been observed.

Stress case: model-call cost doubles from US$3 to US$6; total variable cost becomes US$11; contribution becomes US$9 or 45%. This is a scenario, not a forecast. The largest commercial gap is willingness to pay, not the arithmetic.

Experiment card: In seven days, the founder may test a draft offer with five eligible operators, within an agreed US$50 research budget. Proposed decision rule to confirm before starting: at least two operators request a paid pilot at the stated price, with no unresolved data-access issue. Record the count and exact conditions. A request is not revenue or retention. No outreach or spending has been carried out.

Dependency map: referral source → founder-owned contact record → hosted message workflow → model API → human approval. Proposed fallback: run one fabricated guest request through a manual response process and compare completion time and required records. Unknown result.
